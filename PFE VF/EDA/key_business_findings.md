# Key Business Findings from Initial EDA

## 1. MyTT App Adoption Impact
Customers without the MyTT app are **6.4x more likely to churn** than those with the app. Churn rates: 5.0% (no app) vs. 0.8% (with app).

## 2. Data Usage Decline as Warning Signal
**12.0%** of churners showed a significant data usage drop (>40%) in the month before churning, suggesting disengagement precedes formal churn.

## 3. Revenue Decline Pattern
**61.5%** of churners experienced a substantial ARPU decline (>30%) in their final month, indicating reduced activity before departure.

## 4. Critical Tenure Window
The **<6 months** tenure segment shows the highest churn rate at **9.1%**, highlighting a critical vulnerability period in the customer lifecycle.

## 5. Service Call Impact
Customers who contacted customer service are **0.8x more likely to churn** (2.3% vs. 2.7%), suggesting service issues or unresolved problems drive departures.
